Tóm tắt kiến thức Unit 7–9 (xem nhanh trước khi làm)

Unit 7 · Trips

  • Quá khứ đơn: did + S + V?, didn't + V
  • Phương tiện: ferry, tram, coach, metro, taxi, on foot
  • love / like / don't mind / hate + V-ing
  • Tính từ: crowded, comfortable, convenient, cheap, fast
  • Xin lỗi: Excuse me. / I'm sorry. / That's OK.
  • after, when, while; WORDPOWER get (get home, get up)

Unit 8 · Fit and healthy

  • can / can't (hiện tại), could / couldn't (quá khứ)
  • do yoga · go running · play tennis
  • have to (bắt buộc) · don't have to (không cần)
  • Cơ thể: hands, feet, knees, ears…
  • Sức khỏe: headache, stomach ache · I'm sorry to hear that.
  • Nối câu: and, but, so, because · However, · tell sb / say · trạng từ cách thức (carefully)

Unit 9 · Clothes and shopping

  • Hiện tại tiếp diễn: am/is/are + V-ing (đang làm)
  • Hiện tại đơn (thói quen) vs tiếp diễn (bây giờ)
  • Quần áo: coat, jacket, jumper, sandals, shorts
  • Tiền: price, change, receipt, discount, cash, card
  • Can I try it on? · Here you are. · Can I pay by card?
  • Khen: I love your jacket! – Thanks! · WORDPOWER time (have a good time)
